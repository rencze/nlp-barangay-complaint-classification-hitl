\chapter{Audit Status and Reproducibility Artifacts}
The released dataset contains \texttt{complaint\_text}, \texttt{classification}, language-group allocation, category IDs, source references, scenario and template family identifiers, generation seed and split assignment. These additional fields support audits but must be \emph{excluded} from the primary TF-IDF predictive features. The corpus is marked \texttt{synthetic\_researcher\_constructed\_unreviewed}; generated-language and human-label validation flags remain ``No''. The archived run outputs document all directly reported numbers in Chapter 4. Further human reviews, prospective HITL retraining or production benchmark data are not included because no completed results were provided for those procedures.
